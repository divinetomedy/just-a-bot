Compact pill label for topic categories, status indicators, and counts.

```jsx
<Badge color="blue">Science</Badge>
<Badge color="green" dot>Active</Badge>
<Badge color="orange" size="sm">New</Badge>
<Badge color="purple">Space</Badge>
<Badge color="yellow">History</Badge>
<Badge color="pink" size="lg">Animals</Badge>
<Badge color="neutral">12 facts</Badge>
```

Notable variants/props:
- `color`: blue | green | orange | yellow | purple | pink | neutral | success | warning | danger
- `size`: sm | md | lg
- `dot`: prepends a colored status dot
- All badges are pill-shaped, font is semibold with wide tracking
